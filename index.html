05 Recurring commitments

Know what's already spoken for.

Subscriptions, rent, bills and other regular payments can be set up once and reviewed whenever you need. Upcoming commitments stay visible — without being mistaken for money already spent.

  • Monthly and annual view

    See what your active rules add up to over a month and a year, estimated from their frequency.

  • Upcoming payments

    Know what's due next, with local reminders before it lands.

  • Pause, resume, skip

    Life changes. Adjust a rule without losing track of the commitment.

  • A rule is not an expense

    Recurring rules represent future commitments. They only count as spending when you record the actual payment.

06 Savings, without judgement

Find opportunities. You decide what matters.

The app can point out patterns worth a second look — a category that crept up, a run of small purchases, a commitment you may have forgotten. It won't call your spending wasteful. That's your call.

  • Identify low-value spending

    Detects frequent micro-payments (₹20 – ₹100) and calculates potential savings at a glance.

  • Micro-goals with real impact

    Suggests simple, bite-sized actions — like skipping a few small purchases a week to save ~₹2,480.

  • 3-month baseline comparisons

    Flags when categories or memberships drift higher than your normal average so you can take action.

Savings figures are estimates based on your recorded spending, not promises. Every expense stays yours to judge.

08 Questions

The short answers.

What the app does, where your data lives, and how the details work.

Does Expense Tracker work without internet?

Yes. Recording expenses, monthly targets, local analytics, recurring rules and reminders all work offline. Backups and exports are created on your device. Optional online assistance, if introduced, will be opt-in.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no mandatory sign-in or cloud profile. Your personas and records are stored locally on your Android device.

Where is my data stored?

In the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded automatically. You can create a JSON backup or export PDF and CSV files whenever you choose.

What are personas?

Separate financial contexts — Personal, Business and Tour — each with its own expenses, targets, recurring rules and insights. One persona is active at a time, and switching never merges or deletes the others.

How do monthly targets work?

Targets are set per persona and per calendar month. A target created mid-month applies to that whole month, including expenses recorded earlier. Targets don't carry forward automatically, and there are no rolling 30-day or custom 8th-to-8th cycles.

Is AI used to analyse my spending?

The core insights are calculated locally on your device. Optional online AI assistance is a future direction: it would be opt-in, require explicit consent, and send only minimised summaries — never your full database. Factual figures would still come from local calculations.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes, through a local JSON backup and restore. Core features don't rely on cloud sync.
